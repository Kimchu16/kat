# kat
AA assignment 2026 - **Kimberly Libranza C22386221**
--

Kat is a feline lifeform, with the instincts, personality, and quirks of a playful cat. With a mind of its own, Kat explores its environment, reacts to the player's interactions, and learns over time to develop its own personality. Is it independent or affectionate? Playful or aloof? It's up to you to figure out how to engage with Kat and shape its behaviour.

## Key Features

- **Behavior Trees:** Used to define Kat's emotional reactions and decision-making process, like being curious, playful, or sleepy.
- **Finite State Machine:** Manages Kat's states (e.g. idle, exploring, interacting with objects, resting, or playing).
- **Procedural Animations:** Smooth animations to mimic Kat's natural cat movements like stretching, napping, pouncing, and walking.
- **Inverse Kinematics:** Used to make Kat's limb movements look realistic when interacting with objects or players (e.g., grabbing a toy, rubbing against you).
- **Autonomy and Personality:** Kat has its own desires whether it's seeking food, following you around, or just chilling. It will form a personality based on its interactions with the player.
- **Sound Design:** Cute, cat-like sounds that reflect Kat's mood, such as purring when happy, meowing when curious, or hissing when mad.
- **AI Interactions:** Kat will react to the player's behavior. If you ignore it, it could become distant or annoyed, but if you pet it or play with it, it'll become more affectionate.

## Gameplay

1. **Create Kat:** Choose Kat’s appearance (colour, style, name). Kat's personality starts neutral and evolves based on your interactions.
2. **Let Kat Explore:** Release Kat into its environment and watch it interact with its surroundings. It could explore, sleep, or react to things in the room.
3. **Feed & Care for Kat:** Provide food and care to satisfy Kat’s needs. If neglected, it might get cranky and start avoiding you, but if treated well, it’ll become more affectionate and interactive.
4. **Play with Kat:** Use toys or objects to entertain Kat. It could chase a ball, pounce on things, or even do a little trick to impress you.
5. **Form a Bond:** Depending on how you treat Kat, it’ll either grow attached to you, act like a loyal companion, or become independent and do its own thing.
